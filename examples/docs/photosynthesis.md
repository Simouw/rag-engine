# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae and some bacteria
convert light energy into chemical energy. It takes place mainly in the
chloroplasts of plant cells.

## Light reaction

The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes. They absorb
photons, split water molecules, release oxygen, and produce ATP and NADPH.

## Calvin cycle

The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into glucose. It does
not require light directly and takes place in the stroma.

## Why it matters

Photosynthesis is the entry point of most food chains and is responsible for the
oxygen-rich atmosphere we breathe.
